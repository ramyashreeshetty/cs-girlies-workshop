Strip the branding off and it's the same spine underneath

Overnight HRV is the primary signal in roughly 86% of them. Resting heart rate and sleep sit on top. Nearly all of them track those three.

WHOOP

HRV during deep sleep, one clean reading. 30 day personal baseline. Strain 0 to 21, recovery as a percentage.

OURA

HRV sampled across the whole night. Adds body temperature and sleep debt, which is how it spots illness early.

GARMIN

Body Battery 0 to 100, updated all day. Training Readiness blends six inputs including a 7 day HRV trend and real training load.

APPLE WATCH

No readiness score of its own at all. People bolt on a third party app to get one.

Nothing exotic. Four cheap signals and a good personal baseline. The hard part is still what counts as normal for this person.

And a product decision hiding inside a health metric: WHOOP, Oura and Fitbit Daily Readiness are all subscription only. Garmin and Samsung give it away free.

Dropout is the biggest problem in this whole space. Most of your users will be gone. An app nobody opens helps nobody, so engagement isn't a dirty word.

And also

Anhedonia. People who are depressed find rewards less motivating. Your streak is weakest on the users you built it for.

Deletion. Runners removed sessions from STRAVA that felt too slow. Your users are deleting rows in production, and not at random.

Gamification meta-analysis, JMIR Mental Health 2021. Club runners study, Behavioral Sciences 2026.
